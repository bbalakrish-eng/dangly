Questions

Straight answers about how it behaves on your screen.

Will it get in the way of my clicks?

No. Everything outside the charm is click-through, so you can keep working as normal. Only the charm itself picks up the mouse, so you can drag or flick it.

Does it slow my computer down?

It draws and animates the charm all the time, so it uses some processor and battery. We haven't published figures, and the amount depends on your computer and what else is running. You can hide it whenever you like.

How do I hide it?

Use the menu bar or system tray icon to show, hide or switch charms. Control+Shift+D hides and shows it from anywhere.

Can I try it first?

Yes. Every charm, ritual, weather effect and pet on this page runs live in your browser, on the same engine the app uses.

What are rituals?

Small interactions with a tradition behind them. Break a coconut, light a lamp: click, and it plays out on your screen.

Will I be luckier?

We can't promise that. The charms come from real traditions, and they're meant as a small, personal decoration for your screen.

Where are my settings kept?

Your choice of charm is stored on your own computer.
