Quick answer. Dangly is free on macOS 14 or newer and on Windows 10 or newer. The Mac build is universal and runs natively on both Apple Silicon and Intel, so there's nothing to choose. macOS

Needs: macOS 14 or newer, Apple Silicon or Intel

A universal build: one download runs natively on Apple Silicon and on Intel Macs alike, nothing to choose.

First launch

macOS will say it can't verify the developer, since Dangly isn't a notarized build. Right-click the app in Applications and choose Open, then confirm once. It won't ask again after that.

Updates

Press Check for Updates in Settings and it'll tell you if a newer version is out, with a button straight to it.

Windows

Needs: Windows 10 or newer, 64 bit Intel or AMD

Covers nearly every Windows PC in use today.

First launch

SmartScreen may show a blue "Windows protected your PC" panel, since the build isn't code-signed yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

Updates

Same as macOS: Check for Updates in Settings tells you what's new and opens it for you, nothing runs on its own.

Questions

Is Dangly free?

Yes, on both macOS and Windows. No account, no trial and no paid tier.

Does it work on an Intel Mac?

Yes. The single macOS build is universal and runs natively on both Apple Silicon and Intel, so it's the same download either way.

Will it slow my computer down?

No. It's a lightweight overlay that sits on top of your other windows and stays click-through outside Rage Room mode.

My antivirus or SmartScreen flagged it. What now?

Expected for now, since Dangly isn't code-signed yet. The warning is about that, not about anything the app does. The install guide covers the exact prompts on each platform.
